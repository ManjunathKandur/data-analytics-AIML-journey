* Mathematical operations
* Aggregations
* `np.sum()`
* `np.mean()`
* `np.min()`

* `axis=0` and `axis=1`

* Performed mathematical operations on NumPy arrays
* Calculated total, average, minimum, and maximum values

Created a NumPy-based sales analysis to calculate:

* Total sales
* Average daily sales
* Minimum sales
* Maximum sales
